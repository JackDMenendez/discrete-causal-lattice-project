## Summary
**win-cross-dev-env v0.4.0 is released and live in PROD** (`c:\prod\wcde`) as of
2026-09-04. It carries two new VS Code flavors (`write`, `drawio`) and closes
three environment gaps reported from neighbouring sessions. Four open handoffs
are consumed by this work. Two findings matter beyond the release: **the PM's
v0.2.0 request was based on stale version state and was not executed as
written**, and **the claim that per-profile extension isolation might not be real
is refuted with evidence** — that gate is lifted.

## Decisions & flags
See frontmatter. The two that must not be skimmed: **v0.2.0 was never a real
target**, so any downstream record saying wcde is pre-0.2 is wrong and will
mis-sequence the next release; and **isolation is real**, so the 2026-08-21 gate
that told sessions not to rely on per-profile extension sets should be lifted
rather than propagated. The third — that the draw.io version numbers were
measured against an extension launcher-started windows never load — is the kind
of thing that gets rediscovered as a mystery, which is why it is now a caveat in
the README next to the numbers themselves.
